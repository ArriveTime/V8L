Vital Safety

Know where to turn.

V8L is not an emergency service. These resources can help you locate appropriate official information.

🛡️ Emergency Basics

In the United States, call 911 for an immediate life-threatening emergency.
When traveling internationally, learn the local emergency numbers before you need them.
Keep emergency contacts and important travel information accessible.
Follow official local authorities during severe weather, disasters or evacuations.

🌎 Traveling?

Search for emergency numbers for the country you are visiting. Always verify emergency information with an official government or local authority source.
